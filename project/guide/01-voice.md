# Voice & content

Ramin writes the way he would talk to a client across the table the day after a crash: calm, direct, specific, in the first person. The site is his, so it says “I”. The firm is “RTM Law” (first mention “RTM Law, APC”). The reader is “you”, one person, often hurting and stressed.

## Tone by moment

| Moment | Tone | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Homepage hero | Assured, warm | “Serious injuries deserve personal attention.” |
| Practice page intro | Knowledgeable, practical | “Truck cases turn on records the trucking company controls. I move to preserve them in the first week.” |

| Legal notice | Neutral, complete | “Past results do not guarantee a similar outcome.” |

## Mechanics

- **Sentence case** for every headline, button, nav item and label: “Request a free consultation”, not “Request A Free Consultation”.
- **Uppercase** only in the `eyebrow` style and the logo descriptor, applied with CSS, never typed in caps.
- **Numbers**: numerals for 10 and up and for all money, ages, dates and times. Money in full with commas: “$1,850,000”, never “$1.85M” or “7 figures”.
- **Phone**: `(855) 786-9467`. Link it with `tel:+18557869467`.
- **Dates**: “Sep 18, 2026” in cards; “September 18, 2026” in running text.
- **Places**: freeway names the way Southern Californians say them: “the 5”, “the 405”, “the 55”.
- **Punctuation**: curly quotes and apostrophes (’ “ ”), en dash for ranges (2025–2027), the section sign with a space (§ 335.1). No exclamation marks. No emoji.
- **Names in testimonials**: first name and last initial (“Maria G.”), with written permission.

## Words

| Use | Avoid | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| personal injury attorney | injury specialist, expert | “Specialist” is restricted in California unless certified (Rule 7.4). |
| I represent, I handle | we fight, we battle | Calm over loud. |
| recover compensation | maximum compensation, top dollar | Implies a promised outcome. |
| free consultation | free case evaluation guaranteed | Nothing is guaranteed. |
| verified results | record-breaking, millions recovered | Use the actual figure with the disclaimer, or nothing. |
| careless, negligent | evil, reckless corporations | Facts carry the weight. |
| Super Lawyers®, selected 2025–2027 | award-winning, top-rated | Name the recognition and its years. |
| contingency fee (explained) | no win, no fee | Fee language must say whether costs are owed. |

## Legal terms, explained once

The first time a term appears on a page, explain it in the same sentence: “a contingency fee — you pay attorney fees only if we recover compensation”, “the statute of limitations, the deadline to file”, “a demand letter, the formal request to the insurer”.

## Spanish

The firm serves Spanish-speaking clients (“Hablamos español”). Spanish copy is written by a fluent speaker, not machine-translated, and keeps the same tone using *usted*. Mark every Spanish run with `lang="es"`. The header link reads “Español”; the Spanish site’s link back reads “English”.
